Race across rooftops, clear gaps, throw flips in the air, and land clean to build your score.

How to play

  • ◀ / ▶ — run left and right.
  • JUMP — jump. You can still jump for a split second after leaving an edge.
  • BACK FLIP / FRONT FLIP — tap while airborne to start rotating.
  • KICK FLIP — flip the board under you. Stack it with body flips for bigger combos.
  • Land upright to bank the trick. A bad landing is a bail.
  • Reach the glowing finish gate to complete the run.

Desktop controls: A/D, Space, Q backflip, E frontflip, F kickflip.
